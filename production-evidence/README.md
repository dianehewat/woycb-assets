# Production Evidence

This folder contains evidence that **What Only You Can Bring™** is operating as a live AI-native Human Potential Recognition™ system using **Google Gemini 2.5 Pro via the Gemini API**.

The evidence traces the working system from participant story submission through AI reasoning, Reflection generation and delivery, to real participant feedback and pilot validation.

## Make.com Production Execution Evidence

The live **Human Potential Reflection™ production workflow** was executed through Make.com during the pilot.

The evidence in this folder provides both visual and source-data evidence of the workflow operating in production.

### Processing Time Evidence

The exported Make.com production log contains **33 executions**, including **25 successful executions and 8 error executions**.

Across the **25 successful executions**:

- **Average scenario execution time:** 43.2 seconds
- **Median scenario execution time:** 41.9 seconds
- **Fastest successful execution:** 35.1 seconds
- **Slowest successful execution:** 67.7 seconds

These figures represent **Make.com's recorded scenario execution duration**.

They do **not** represent the complete participant-perceived elapsed time from pressing **Continue** in Tally to receiving the final email, as final delivery also depends on services outside the recorded Make.com scenario duration.

From the participant perspective, personalised **Human Potential Reflections™ were typically received within minutes of story submission**.

### Why the Full Execution History Is Included

The production evidence intentionally includes both successful and unsuccessful executions rather than presenting only selected successful runs.

The errors form part of the genuine development and operational history of the system.

The successful executions demonstrate the repeatable operation of the automated production workflow, while the complete history provides a transparent record of testing, troubleshooting and live execution during the pilot.

### What This Evidence Demonstrates

The execution history provides evidence that the Human Potential Reflection™ workflow was not simply a proposed architecture.

It was a **working automated production system** used during the pilot.

Within the production workflow:

**Tally** | Participant story  
↓  
**Make.com** | Webhook + orchestration  
↓  
**Google Sheets** | Participant + workflow record  
↓  
**Gemini 2.5 Pro via Gemini API** | Human Potential Recognition™ constitutional reasoning  
↓  
**Make.com** | Structured output processing  
↓  
**Google Docs** | Personalised Human Potential Reflection™  
↓  
**Google Drive** | Storage  
↓  
**Gmail** | Participant delivery
