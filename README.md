# What Only You Can Bring™

## Build with Gemini XPRIZE | Technical Repository

**What Only You Can Bring™ is a live AI-native implementation of Human Potential Recognition™, built with Google Gemini 2.5 Pro.**

The working participant experience is orchestrated through Make.com, with **Google Gemini 2.5 Pro via the Gemini API** performing the core Human Potential Recognition™ reasoning.

## Core AI Implementation

Gemini 2.5 Pro receives the participant's unstructured lived story together with instructions governed by the **Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution**.

Gemini reasons across the story to recognise meaningful patterns of observable human contribution, evaluates the evidence available within the participant's story, and thoughtfully hypothesises the human potential those patterns may reveal.

The resulting structured output is transformed into a personalised **Human Potential Reflection™** and delivered to the participant through the automated workflow.

**Make.com moves and orchestrates the information. Gemini performs the reasoning.**

## Human Oversight

Gemini has autonomy over the reasoning required to create each Reflection within defined constitutional boundaries.

Human oversight operates at the system level through the design and evolution of the **Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution**, workflow monitoring, participant feedback, evaluation and continuous improvement.

Gemini does not diagnose participants, assign fixed personality labels, score human potential or determine what participants should believe about themselves.

**Gemini owns the reasoning task. The Constitution governs the boundaries. The participant owns the meaning.**

## Privacy

The Make.com blueprint in this repository is a sanitised submission copy. Credentials, participant information and private resource identifiers are not included.

All public production evidence has been reviewed to protect participant privacy and exclude credentials, API keys, private resource identifiers and other sensitive information.
