# What Only You Can Bring™

## Build with Gemini XPRIZE | Technical Repository

**What Only You Can Bring™** is an AI-native, low-code implementation of **Human Potential Recognition™**.

The working participant experience is orchestrated through Make.com, with **Google Gemini 2.5 Pro via the Gemini API** performing the core Human Potential Recognition™ reasoning.

## Live Workflow

**Tally** | Participant story  
↓  
**Make.com** | Webhook + orchestration  
↓  
**Google Sheets** | Participant + workflow record  
↓  
**Gemini 2.5 Pro via Gemini API** | Constitutional reasoning  
↓  
**Google Docs** | Human Potential Reflection™  
↓  
**Google Drive** | Storage  
↓  
**Gmail** | Participant delivery  
↓  
**Tally + Make.com** | Feedback

## Core AI Implementation

Gemini 2.5 Pro receives the participant's unstructured lived story together with instructions governed by the **Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution**.

Gemini reasons across the story to recognise meaningful patterns of observable human contribution and thoughtfully hypothesise the human potential those patterns may reveal.

The resulting structured output is transformed into a personalised **Human Potential Reflection™** and delivered to the participant through the automated workflow.

## Human Oversight

Gemini has autonomy over the reasoning required to create each reflection within defined constitutional boundaries.

**Gemini owns the reasoning task. The Constitution governs the boundaries. The participant owns the meaning.**

## Privacy

The Make.com blueprint in this repository is a sanitised submission copy. Credentials, participant information and private resource identifiers are not included.
