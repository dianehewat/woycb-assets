# Gemini 2.5 Pro — Human Potential Recognition™ Reasoning

## Role

Google Gemini 2.5 Pro is the production LLM and core reasoning engine used by **What Only You Can Bring™**.

The live Make.com workflow calls Gemini to transform an unstructured participant story into a personalised **Human Potential Reflection™**.

## Reasoning Approach

Gemini is guided by the **Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution**, which establishes the principles and boundaries through which it reasons.

For each participant story, Gemini is asked to:

1. reason across unstructured lived experience;
2. recognise meaningful patterns of observable human contribution;
3. determine which interpretations are sufficiently grounded in the participant's story;
4. thoughtfully hypothesise the human potential those patterns may reveal;
5. synthesise those insights into a deeply personalised Human Potential Reflection™; and
6. generate reflective questions grounded in the participant's lived experience.

## Constitutional Boundaries

The approach is designed to:

- recognise rather than assess;
- preserve participant agency;
- communicate with humility;
- avoid fixed labels;
- avoid scoring human potential;
- avoid comparison against population norms; and
- return ownership of meaning to the participant.

Gemini does not make employment, financial, clinical or other consequential decisions about participants.

## Submission-Safe Note

This repository documents the reasoning architecture used during the hackathon. It intentionally does not publish the full proprietary Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution or private participant data.

**Gemini provides the reasoning. The Human Potential Recognition™ Constitution provides the boundaries. The participant's lived experience provides the evidence.**
